Where AI is used today

AI is rarely a product on its own. It gets built into something that already exists, so the useful question is which part of the work it takes over.

A few common examples of AI in everyday work
Field What the AI does
Health care Marks suspicious areas on a scan for a doctor to check
Transport Spots lanes, signs and obstacles in real time
Factories Finds broken parts on a line faster than a person can look
Language Drafts, summarises and translates text

Problems to watch for

  • Bias: a model learns from data, so it also copies the unfairness in that data.
  • Made up answers: language models produce the most likely next words, not checked facts, so anything important has to be checked somewhere else.
  • Privacy: text sent to a chat tool may be stored, so passwords and personal details do not belong in a prompt.
